# 🏥 Healthcare Data Analysis Dashboard

📌 Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive analysis of a massive healthcare dataset, totaling $1.42 Billion in Revenue. The dashboard is designed to monitor hospital operations, patient diagnostics, and pharmaceutical trends to drive data-driven decision-making for healthcare administrators and providers.

## 📊 Key Sections & Insights

1. Executive KPIs
Total Revenue: $1.42 Billion.

Scale: Analysis covers 39,876 Hospitals and 40,341 Doctors.

Patient Volume: Total of 40,235 Patients currently tracked in the system.

2. Operational Patient Traffic
Mid-Week Peak: Traffic is highest on Wednesday (6,840 patients) and gradually declines toward Monday (6,756 patients).

Staffing Insight: Resource allocation should be prioritized for mid-week surges to maintain service quality.

3. Financial Performance
Monthly Wise Revenue: Identifies peak earnings in July ($122.76M) and a significant performance dip in February ($107.89M).

Top Contributors: Johnson PLC leads the network with $1.08M in revenue.

4. Clinical & Diagnostic Analysis
Medical Conditions: Even distribution among chronic conditions such as Diabetes (16.93%), Hypertension (16.78%), and Asthma (16.61%).

Test Results: A critical finding shows that 46% of patients (18,627) have Abnormal test results, highlighting a high clinical workload.

5. Supply Chain & Insurance
Medication Usage: High demand for Lipitor (20.09%) and Ibuprofen (20.01%).

Insurance Providers: Cigna and Medicare are the top providers, each covering over 9,400 providers.
